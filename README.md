# Zizzet CRM

Zizzet is an AI-powered customer engagement and revenue recovery platform that helps businesses capture leads, automate follow-ups, recover missed enquiries, and convert conversations into confirmed bookings.

### Key Features
- Automated lead recovery and follow-ups
- AI-powered voice and conversational engagement
- WhatsApp, SMS, and customer reactivation
- Automated appointment booking and reminders
- CRM pipeline and revenue tracking
